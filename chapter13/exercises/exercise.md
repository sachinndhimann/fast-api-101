# Exercise

List five operations in an e-commerce checkout flow (charge payment, reserve inventory, send
confirmation email, notify the warehouse, update a recommendation model). For each, decide synchronous
vs event-driven, and justify in one sentence.

## Interview-style scenario

Your team wants to move "charge the customer's card" to a background queue to make checkout feel
faster. What could go wrong with that specific choice, and why is "reserve inventory" a better first
candidate to move to a queue?
